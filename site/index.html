What it does

Seven screens, and none of them ask you to sign up.

Everything below works with no account, offline, on the plane. Tick them off as you go.

Watch Next

Your backlog, already sorted

Three groups — up next, haven't watched in a while, haven't started. Tap the circle to mark an episode watched. The same list runs as a home-screen widget.

Library

Shows and movies in one place

A poster grid with progress bars, four sort orders, and filters for Watching, Up to date, Not started, Finished and Archived. Movies and documentaries get their own watchlist.

Upcoming

Air dates, as a list or a calendar

Every announced episode from the shows you follow, by day with air time and network, or as a month calendar. Alerts are scheduled on your phone, with no server involved.

Episodes

Swipe through a season

Still, air date and synopsis on a full page, with spoilers hidden until you ask. Mark it watched, tap the check again to log a rewatch, and rate it from half a star to five.

Discover

One search box, four rails

Search TV, movies and documentaries in one place. Below that: recommendations built from the cast, creators and genres you already watch, plus premiering soon, all-time greats and airing tonight.

Friends · optional

The social part is opt-in

Sign in with Apple to follow friends, see what they watched and rated, and join the comment thread on an episode — with community ratings and a best-character vote. Skip it and the app is unchanged.

Profile

How much time it actually took

Your profile totals the months, days and hours you've watched, plus your most-watched shows and top genres. The diary lists every episode, day by day.

Your data

It lives on your phone, not on someone's server.

Your library — every show, episode, watch date, rating and movie — is a single database file on your iPhone, and the whole app works signed out.

No tracking, at all

No analytics SDK, no ad network, no crash reporter, no tracking of any kind, and there never has been.

Signing in is a choice

Sign in with Apple only turns on the friends layer. From then on the episodes you mark watched are mirrored so the people who follow you can see them.

Comments are public

Episode comments and any photo or GIF you attach are visible to other signed-in users, so treat those as public.

Leaving is one tap

Delete your account from Settings and everything on the server goes with it. The library on your device stays exactly where it is.

Artwork and air dates are fetched from TVmaze and Apple whether or not you sign in, so some requests always leave your phone. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Get it

The beta is open on TestFlight.

It's one person's iPhone app and it's free. If you have an export from another TV tracking app, you can import it on the first run.
