3. What the App does, and what it does not do

Local by default. Your library — the shows and movies you follow, your watch dates, your per-episode ratings, your play counts and your settings — is stored in a database on your own device. You can use the entire tracking side of the App without ever creating an account: following shows, marking episodes watched, rating them, tracking movies and documentaries, local new-episode notifications, the home-screen widget, importing an export from another TV tracking app and exporting a backup file all work signed out.

Signing in is optional. Sign in with Apple is the only sign-in method, and it exists solely to unlock the social features: your public profile, following people, the activity feed, community episode ratings, "friends who watched", best-character votes and episode comments.

What signing in shares. When you are signed in, the episodes you mark watched and the ratings you give them are mirrored to our backend so that people who follow you can see them, and so the App can compute community averages and recommendations. When you first sign in, your existing local library is backfilled to the server for the same reason. Comments you post are visible to every signed-in user, not only to your followers. Photos you attach to comments are stored in a publicly-readable location and are retrievable by anyone who has the URL, even without an account. Do not post a photo you would not be comfortable being publicly accessible.

Ratings are per-episode. The App rates individual episodes. There is no show-level rating and no private notes or review feature.

What the App does not do. The App does not play video, does not link to or provide streaming, does not tell you where a title is available to stream, and does not offer direct messages. Movies and documentaries are tracked locally only and have no social layer. There is no cloud restore of your library — moving to a new phone means exporting a backup file yourself.

Not a backup service. We are not a storage or backup provider for your library. Your library lives on your device. Deleting the App, losing the device, or a device failure can lose it permanently. Export a backup file if it matters to you.
