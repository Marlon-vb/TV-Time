# What's New

Draft for the next App Store update. Version and build numbers stay where they are until that release is cut.

New episode alerts

Get a notification when a new episode of a show you follow is out. Turn it on in Settings. Archived shows stay quiet, and the app asks for notification permission only when you turn alerts on.

Where to watch

Show and movie pages list the streaming services that include the title, plus rent and buy options when they exist. The list uses your device region, and streaming data is provided by JustWatch.
